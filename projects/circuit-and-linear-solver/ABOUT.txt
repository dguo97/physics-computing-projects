CIRCUIT AND LINEAR-SYSTEM SOLVER
================================

WHAT IT DOES
Solves sets of simultaneous linear equations, in two examples:
  1. a general 4-equation, 4-unknown system, and
  2. an electrical circuit with 9 unknowns (the currents in the wires and one voltage).

HOW IT WORKS
The circuit rules (current going into a junction equals current coming out; voltages around a loop
add up to zero; Ohm's law V = I x R) all give straight-line equations in the unknowns. These are
written as one matrix equation and solved exactly by the computer.

CHECKING THE ANSWER
The program then tests the solution against extra loop equations that were NOT used in solving. They
all come out as zero, which shows the answer is consistent.

RUN IT
python linear_solver.py            (--which circuit or --which system to run just one)
