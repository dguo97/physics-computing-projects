ENERGY-MIXING SIMULATION
========================

WHAT IT DOES
Simulates 2000 objects sharing energy. Half start with very little (0.1) and half with a lot (1000).
Over and over, two objects chosen at random meet and each ends up with the average of their two
energies.

WHAT HAPPENS
Total energy never changes, so the average stays at 500.05. But the energies spread out evenly and
get closer and closer to that average. After 10,000 meetings the spread has shrunk from about 500 to
about 40.

THE MATHS
On average the variance (spread squared) shrinks by a factor of about e every 2000 meetings. The
program checks this prediction against the simulation.

OUTPUT
- Mean and spread of the energies, printed.
- output/energy_mixing.png - histogram of the energies after the meetings.
- output/convergence.png (from energy_mixing_convergence.py) - simulated spread against the prediction.

RUN IT
python energy_mixing.py            (--collisions 50000 --seed 1 to change it)
python energy_mixing_convergence.py
