MOTION FROM ACCELERATION
========================

WHAT IT DOES
Given how an object's acceleration changes with time, it calculates how fast the object is moving
and how far it has travelled, and gives the numerical error on each answer.

THE SCENARIO
The object speeds up smoothly for about the first 10 seconds, then coasts with no acceleration from
10 s to 40 s, then starts braking at around 47 s.

HOW IT WORKS
Velocity is the area under the acceleration curve; position is the area under the velocity curve.
The program calculates these areas numerically (adaptive numerical integration), which also reports
how accurate each area is. It is told about the moments where the rule for acceleration changes so
it does not lose accuracy there.

OUTPUT
After 50 s: position about 894.4 m and speed about 12.1 m/s, with errors of around a hundred-thousandth
of a metre. The speed is still above zero because braking has only just started.

RUN IT
python motion.py              (add --time 30 to stop at 30 seconds)
