SCHRODINGER SHOOTING METHOD
===========================

WHAT IT DOES
Finds the allowed energy, and the shape of the wave function, of a quantum particle trapped in a box
with walls it cannot pass. The floor of the box is flat in the left half and raised by a step (V0) in
the right half.

HOW IT WORKS ("SHOOTING")
Guess an energy, then follow the wave function from the left wall using fourth-order Runge-Kutta. If
the wave function does not land exactly on zero at the right wall, the guess was wrong. The program
repeatedly halves the range of possible energies (bisection) until the wave function does hit zero at
the right wall. The result is then scaled so that the total probability of finding the particle in
the box is exactly 1.

OUTPUT
- E = 69.1985 eV (for the default step height of 5 eV and a search range of 50 to 100 eV).
- output/schrodinger_wavefunction.png and output/schrodinger_probability.png
- output/convergence.png (from schrodinger_convergence.py) - the answer gets 16 times more accurate
  each time the step size is halved.

RUN IT
python schrodinger.py              (try --v0 8 --e-low 60 --e-high 120)
python schrodinger_convergence.py
