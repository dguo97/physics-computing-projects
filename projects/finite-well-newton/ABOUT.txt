FINITE-WELL ENERGY (NEWTON'S METHOD)
====================================

WHAT IT DOES
Finds an allowed energy of a quantum particle in a region where the potential energy has two levels.
Quantum mechanics only allows certain energies, and they are the answers to an equation that cannot be
rearranged to give the energy directly.

HOW IT WORKS
The program uses Newton's method: start with a guess, then repeatedly improve it using the slope of
the equation, until the guess stops changing. Each step roughly squares the previous error, so it
becomes accurate in only 8 steps.

INPUT
Well depth V0 (default 10 eV), width (default 1,000,000) and a starting guess (default 50 eV, which
must be above V0). A different guess can lead to a different allowed energy.

OUTPUT
E = 51.5930 eV. output/convergence.png (from well_convergence.py) shows the size of each Newton step
dropping extremely fast.

RUN IT
python well_energy.py              (--v0 10 --width 1e6 --guess 70 to change it)
python well_convergence.py
