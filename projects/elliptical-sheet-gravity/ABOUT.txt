ELLIPTICAL SHEET GRAVITY
========================

WHAT IT DOES
Finds the mass of a flat, evenly thick elliptical plate (10 m by 5 m semi-axes) and the gravitational
pull it exerts on a 10 kg object placed near it.

HOW IT WORKS
The plate is treated as a huge number of tiny pieces. The pull from each piece is calculated and
added up using numerical double integration over the ellipse's area.

CHECKS
- The plate's mass comes out at exactly 157,079.6 kg, matching the formula (density x pi x a x b).
- Directly above the middle of the plate the sideways pulls cancel out, and the downward pull is
  towards the plate.
- Very far away, the plate pulls like a single point mass.

RUN IT
python sheet_gravity.py            (--position X Y Z to move the object)
